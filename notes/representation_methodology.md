# Representation methodology

A representation is selected after the target quantity and information structure are fixed.

A candidate must answer:

- What statistical object does it summarize?
- Is it defined at the same level as the target risk or discrepancy?
- Which part is source-estimable?
- Which part is controlled only by an assumption?
- What is irreducible or unidentifiable?
- Can its bound be tight on simple counterexamples?
- Does it require microscopic network details that the theorem does not use?

Prefer exact decompositions and dual forms before inequalities. Distinguish a latent mathematical quantity from an estimator of it. The branch does not import A/O/Pi, Koopman operators, local response geometry, or optimizer trajectories without a later theorem-specific reason.
