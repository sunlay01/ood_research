# Unresolved Bridges

## B1. Regularizer to conditional property

Prove or refute, in a named restricted class, an implication from MMD/CORAL, V-REx, IRMv1 or Fishr values to a conditional operator radius. The Zhao-style counterexample shows this is false without assumptions.

## B2. Source-only target-family coverage

Characterize when `Pi` tails or an operator uncertainty radius can be estimated from finitely many source domains. Otherwise retain `q_delta` or misspecification as an irreducible term.

## B3. Derivative-class concentration

For IRMv1/Fishr, define a measurable bounded derivative class and quantify empirical-to-population translation plus stationarity error. Deep unrestricted classes are expected to be vacuous.

## B4. Conditional operator theorem

Under overlap and bounded loss, prove a marginal-plus-conditional change-of-measure bound and identify when TV/Dobrushin contraction or an SCM makes the conditional remainder zero.

## B5. Comparison theorem

Under matched target families, compare a V-REx risk-dispersion certificate with finite-mixture DRO and an IPM certificate. The comparison must be about bound terms, not benchmark performance.

Current status: these are targeted proof/search tasks, not evidence for a universal representation.
