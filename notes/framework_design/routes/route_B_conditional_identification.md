- **Problem instance:** P3, ideal IRM/IRMv1 to intervention-family risk.
- **Formalization:** SCM or invariant conditional framework with rank/coverage assumptions.
- **Reuse/change:** reuse ICP/anchor/ideal-IRM identification calculus; practical gradients are translated locally.
- **Representation:** conditional mechanism or identified parent set, not a new universal feature tuple.
- **Required result:** restricted empirical derivative/stationarity lemma to the ideal conditional property.
- **Assumptions:** valid SCM, faithfulness/positivity, intervention heterogeneity, smoothness and derivative complexity.
- **Source-estimable:** source conditional tests and derivative statistics.
- **Assumption-controlled:** graph, intervention family, rank and coverage.
- **Irreducible:** non-identifiability under weak heterogeneity and optimization/translation error.
- **Failure case:** finite environments admit noncausal IRM solutions.
- **Solved if successful:** a restricted, mechanism-level IRMv1 result with honest fidelity labels.
