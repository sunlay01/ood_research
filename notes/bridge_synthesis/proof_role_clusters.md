# Proof-role clusters

## 1. Source-target distinguishability

Objects: HΔH, loss-class discrepancy, RKHS MMD, covariance moments. Incoming edge is empirical-process estimation; outgoing edge is a change-of-measure or discrepancy inequality. Target dependence or a target-family assumption is unavoidable.

## 2. Shared mechanism identification

Objects: invariant conditional law, simultaneous optimum, SCM parent set, rank/diversity. Incoming edge is causal/invariance assumption or rank argument; outgoing edge is intervention-stable prediction. These objects are not interchangeable with marginal IPMs.

## 3. Admissible target uncertainty

Objects: f-divergence/Wasserstein balls, finite-group simplex, domain meta-law `Pi`. Incoming edge is a modeling/coverage assumption; outgoing edge is a support function, robust risk, or domain concentration theorem.

## 4. Shift contraction/localization

Objects: Dobrushin coefficient, localized discrepancy, direct-sum invariant/variant terms. Incoming edge is conditional invariance or decomposition; outgoing edge contracts raw shift into target error.

## 5. Algorithm-to-population translation

Objects: TV variation functional, population IRM constraint, Taylor meta-objective, derivative class. Incoming edge is variational translation, approximation, or optimization assumption; outgoing edge is a population bridge only in restricted models.

## 6. Statistical estimation

Objects: VC/Rademacher complexity, Hilbert concentration, domain-level complexity, information radius. Incoming edge is sampling; outgoing edge is finite-sample deviation. Complexity and boundedness are not OOD semantics.

## 7. Irreducible ambiguity

Objects: joint-error oracle, conditional mismatch, target tail, misspecification radius, minimax indistinguishability. These are the terms preventing arbitrary-target claims.
