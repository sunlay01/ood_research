# Problem formulation

The first theory milestone is to choose one explicit quantity of interest. Candidate formulations are:

1. excess target risk `R_T(f) - R_T(f_T^*)`;
2. transfer gap `R_T(f) - R_S(f)`;
3. worst-case source-defined risk `sup_{P in P_adm} R_P(f)`;
4. an impossibility statement for source-only prediction of target risk.

For each candidate, record the information set, admissible target family, loss assumptions, hypothesis class, and whether the quantity is estimable from source data. Do not call a bound informative until its irreducible and shift-uncertainty terms are visible.
