# Zhao2019 / On Learning Invariant Representations for Domain Adaptation

## Problem
Determine when feature alignment can help or hurt target prediction under label and conditional shifts.

## Target
Target risk / source-target risk gap with an explicit label-distribution conflict term.

## Representation
Joint feature-distribution alignment plus conditional-label/joint-error quantities.

## Proof bottleneck
Marginal feature matching discards label information and cannot identify target conditionals by itself.

## Proof-enabling property
Change-of-measure decomposition exposes the unavoidable tradeoff between domain discrepancy and label predictability.

## Main theorem
Theorem 1 gives a lower/upper relationship showing that alignment and target error are coupled through conditional and label-distribution terms; exact constants depend on the stated hypotheses.

## Price of tractability
Distributional/representation assumptions and an irreducible joint-error term; this is a diagnostic result, not a source-only upper bound.

## Algorithm relationship
NOT-ALGORITHM-SPECIFIC; use as an insufficiency test for MMD/CORAL/DANN-style unifiers.

## Scope
Naturally covered: feature alignment and DA impossibility analysis. Excluded: claims that marginal alignment alone guarantees conditional invariance.

## Source-only status
NO in the DA theorem; target/label terms are target-dependent or oracle.

## Evidence
Theorem 1; ICML/PMLR 97.
