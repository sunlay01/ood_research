# Lai2024 / Invariant Risk Minimization Is A Total Variation Model

## Problem
Give IRMv1's classifier-gradient penalty a functional/statistical interpretation suitable for OOD analysis.

## Target
TV variation of population risk in the classifier variable and resulting OOD/minimax conditions.

## Representation
Total variation (`TV-l2` for the usual squared gradient norm, with a `TV-l1` variant) of `w ↦ R_e(w∘Φ)`.

## Proof bottleneck
The gradient penalty is parameter-level notation, while population OOD theorems need a function-space object.

## Proof-enabling property
Variational/coarea and measure-theoretic arguments turn derivative norms into a total-variation functional and characterize admissible environment sets.

## Main theorem
Theorems 3.1-3.11 establish the TV interpretation and associated OOD/minimax formulations under regularity conditions.

## Price of tractability
Functional regularity, coarea/measure assumptions and explicit environment-family conditions; no generic finite-sample deep-SGD theorem.

## Algorithm relationship
FUNCTIONAL-TRANSLATION, not cross-method unification.

## Scope
Naturally covered: IRM gradient-to-functional analysis. Excluded: GroupDRO, Fishr or generic Wasserstein DRO without an additional map.

## Source-only status
YES for the stated population environment family; operational target guarantees remain conditional.

## Evidence
ICML/PMLR 235; Theorems 3.1-3.11; abstract and Sec. 3.
