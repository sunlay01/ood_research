# Unresolved questions and explicit exclusions

These are retrieval prompts, not claims that the questions have been solved.

1. **Source-only target family.** For a future theorem, what observable source statistic can identify or upper-bound the target-family term without silently importing target samples? The DA discrepancy and joint-error terms remain target-dependent/oracle.
2. **Representation sufficiency.** Can two environments have identical risk vectors or RKHS means while having different conditional label mechanisms? If yes, those representations cannot by themselves support a non-vacuous conditional-shift theorem.
3. **IRM translation scope.** Lai--Wang's TV functional is a mathematically explicit translation of the classifier-variable gradient penalty, but it is OPEN whether a useful finite-sample bound can retain exact deep IRMv1 fidelity.
4. **Optimizer-statistic gap.** Fishr's gradient covariance is not represented by the risk-vector, IPM, DRO, or direct-sum latent languages. A derivative-class concentration theorem and an explicit shift family are still missing here.
5. **Causal versus statistical unification.** The SCM family, INV representation slot, and Tri-Space latent decomposition make different assumptions. Do not merge them unless a formal map preserves the target quantity and proof obligations.
6. **2024--2026 coverage.** The corpus contains verified 2024, 2025 and 2026 representatives, but is not a complete census. Search should continue for papers that introduce a genuinely new theoretical representation rather than another empirical regularizer.
7. **Information-theoretic operationalization.** Liu et al.'s information-radius terms can be non-operational for deterministic deep training. Determine which stochastic learner quantities are source-estimable and which are oracle.
8. **Lower-bound tests.** Wang et al. (2024), Rosenfeld et al. (2021), and Zhao et al. (2019) should be consulted before proposing a unified theorem: preserve their non-identifiability and joint-error failure modes.
