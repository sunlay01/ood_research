# Literature Audit

This track is an algebraic benchmark, not a novelty claim.  Its direct-sum
uniqueness and overlap ambiguity are standard finite-dimensional linear
algebra.  Whitened gradient Gram matrices are standard intrinsic metric
constructions.  The empirical contribution here is only an auditable protocol
that compares response-level module diagnostics under nuisance, redundancy,
coordinate changes and held-out additive probes.

No literature review in this artifact is evidence for causal mechanism
discovery.  Any future comparison with subspace clustering, invariant-risk
methods or shift attribution must preserve the distinction between response
geometry and generating semantics.
