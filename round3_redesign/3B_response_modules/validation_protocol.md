# Validation Protocol

Blind discovery uses only retained whitened response columns or their
intrinsic Gram matrix.  It compares point clustering, local-PCA subspace
reporting, a self-expression-affinity spectral baseline, and the Gram proxy.
Mechanism labels, exposure, target-risk values, and oracle module count are
excluded from selection.

Post-hoc ARI/NMI and mechanism enrichment are descriptive only.  The
pre-registered semantic gate is ARI and NMI at least `0.80`; the present run
does not meet it.  Rotation, nuisance, shortcut, redundancy and mixed-shift
checks remain separate from semantic alignment.
