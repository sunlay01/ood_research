# Literature semantic decomposition audit

The bridge uses operational distinctions established in prior work: invariant
or causal predictors versus environment-specific nuisance (IRM, Arjovsky et
al., 2019), domain-invariant feature statistics (CORAL, Sun & Saenko, 2016),
and meta-learning across domains (MLDG, Li et al., 2018). In CMNIST these map
to label/digit content versus color and label-noise interventions.

No citation is used to select a direction after inspecting target outcomes.
Evaluation-only interventions are not source-observable mechanisms.
