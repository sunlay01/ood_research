## Consequence for this probe

The reusable pattern is source-domain pseudo-holdout plus an exact local derivative in a controlled model. The selected prototype is therefore a finite source-domain response-matching regularizer, not a claim of a new DG family. In particular, the local response target is estimated from source-domain head optima, while `A`, true target moments, target risk, and semantic labels are unavailable to objective construction. Any algebraic coincidence with MLDG/Fish-style derivative matching is reported as a special case rather than presented as novelty.
