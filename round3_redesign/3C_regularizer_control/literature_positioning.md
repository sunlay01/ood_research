# Literature Positioning

The direct constraint-object descriptions are standard method definitions:
L2 penalizes parameter norm, CORAL compares representation covariances, V-REx
penalizes environment-risk dispersion, and IRMv1 uses the scalar classifier
optimality proxy.  These statements are not claimed as novel.

The project-specific analysis is the common response-level comparison:

\[
\text{constraint object}\to(b_j,K_j)\to\text{coverage of }q_s.
\]

The report separates literature-derived definitions, exact quadratic identities,
numerical diagnostics, and counterexamples.  No method is assigned a causal or
latent semantic mechanism.
