# Exposure Audit

For each family and seed we compute `P_ker(O_S)`, `||A P_ker(O_S)||`, and the
information floor `1/2 ||A P_ker(O_S)||^2`.  Hidden and exposed designs share
the generator and representation; only the declared source exposure differs.
The irrelevant-diversity control tests whether extra source state variation
changes raw observation geometry without changing task response.
