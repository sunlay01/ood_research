# Method Comparison

ERM, L2, IRMv1 and V-REx are compared within family and source design.  Each
row reports static steering `||z0||`, adaptive action `||Pi O_S||`, residual
`||E||`, affine regret and excess over the family information floor.  Lambda
is fixed in advance and target metrics are post-hoc only.

CORAL is not included in predictor-space affine rows because its fixed
representation predictor action is noncanonical; the prior CMNIST CORAL probe
remains the separate gauge audit.
