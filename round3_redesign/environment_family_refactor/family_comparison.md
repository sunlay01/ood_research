# Cross-family comparison

The same underlying source problem is evaluated under the legacy Gaussian
mechanism family and the source-induced family.  The latter has a smaller
tangent because it is generated from observed source variation.  Consequently
its zero information floor is a statement about a smaller admissible
uncertainty set, not evidence of universal robustness.

The method table reports L2, IRMv1 and V-REx population affine rows at the same
source optimum.  `Pi O_S`, recoverable residual and affine regret are recomputed
with the family tangent metric.  No semantic module, target oracle, or old 3C
geometry selects a family or a rank.
