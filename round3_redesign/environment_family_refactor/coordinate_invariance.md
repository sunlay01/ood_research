# Coordinate and metric invariance

For a world recoding `u_new=B u_old`, the transported objects are

`A_new=A_old B^-1`, `O_new=O_old B^-1`, and
`G_new=B^(-T) G_old B^-1`.

The metric-aware whitening audit preserves the information floor under this
transport.  Orthogonal world and response isometries are special cases.  A
general invertible recoding without the metric transport is deliberately not
called an invariance test: it changes the declared uncertainty geometry.
