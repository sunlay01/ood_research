# Literature positioning

The abstraction is an interface choice, not a novelty claim.  Source-induced
families are compatible with source-span and moment-alignment viewpoints;
structural intervention families relate to IRM-style causal assumptions;
distributional robustness may instead place a meta-distribution over domains;
and partial transportability studies plausible target families.  This track
only makes the admissible family an explicit argument of the existing local
task/source/algorithm geometry so that family-relative comparisons are
auditable.
