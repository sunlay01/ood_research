# Official CMNIST Reproduction

This panel runs the pinned DomainBed implementations of GroupDRO, ANDMask, SANDMask, and MLDG on ColoredMNIST. Official upstream training is intentionally separate from the local geometry runner.

A low target score is recorded as `unresolved` until protocol, implementation, budget, and checkpoint selection audits are complete.

Results: `results/method_fidelity.csv` and `results/method_fidelity_summary.json`.
